# Authors and Credits

BFA-HavenCore is a community-maintained World of Warcraft emulator for Battle
for Azeroth 8.3.7. It continues work from earlier projects in this lineage:

TrinityCore → LatinoCore → BfaCore-Reforged → BFA-HavenCore

We thank the developers, researchers, testers, and communities who contributed
code, fixes, data, documentation, and research to these projects.

## Project history

- WoW Daemon Team, 2004
- MaNGOS project, 2005–2008, [mangosproject.org](http://www.mangosproject.org)
- MaNGOS project, 2008–2011, [getmangos.com](http://getmangos.com)
- UDB project, 2007–2009, [udbforums.org](http://udbforums.org)
- SD2 project, 2008–2009, [scriptdev2.com](http://www.scriptdev2.com)
- TrinityCore, 2008–2021
- LatinoCore, authors and dates not known
- BfaCore-Reforged, 2021–2022
- BFA-HavenCore, ongoing

These dates describe project eras, not the authorship dates of individual
changes.

## Authorship and attribution

Individual code authorship is recorded in Git history where that history is

provided by the source history.

For code inherited from projects whose full history is not present here, see
their own author records and repositories:

- [TrinityCore authors](https://github.com/TrinityCore/TrinityCore/blob/master/AUTHORS)

## TrinityCore lineage credits

TrinityCore grew from earlier community work, including the MaNGOS project.
We acknowledge Team Python and the WoW Daemon Team, as well as the developers
and communities who contributed patches, bug reports, and research.

Special thanks to the WowwoW team for their help with the project; the
ScriptDev2 team for scripts; and the WCell team, especially Ralek, for research
on realm reconnect sequences, item scaling, and game object rotation.

## BFA-HavenCore contributors
